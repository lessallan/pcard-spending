Pension contributions

The Police and Fire department budgets include $2.04 million for the police pension and $3.18 million for the fire pension, about $5.21 million combined. These contributions are part of the personnel costs above. The city also budgets retirement contributions for other employees.

A contribution is what the city puts into a plan for the year. A pension liability measures obligations over time.

Amendment 3 and your property tax bill

Which taxes does the expanded exemption affect?

The increased exemption applies to eligible non-school property taxes. School taxes and separate assessments can still appear on the bill, and individual savings depend on your assessed value, eligibility and future rates.

What if I moved to Florida after 2026?

Florida’s Department of Revenue says owners who had not established Florida residency by December 31, 2026 generally become eligible for the expanded exemption in their fifth year of exemption. Check your eligibility with the Bay County Property Appraiser.
