Pension contributions

The Police and Fire department budgets include $2.04 million for the police pension and $3.18 million for the fire pension, about $5.21 million combined. These contributions are part of the personnel costs above. The city also budgets retirement contributions for other employees.

A contribution is what the city puts into a plan for the year. A pension liability measures obligations over time.

City Hall

The city borrowed $16.33 million in 2018 for the renovation. The original financing still had $11.05 million outstanding at September 30, 2024. The FY 2027 adopted budget includes $1,419,570 for this note, including $1,146,000 in principal and $273,570 in interest. The separate City Hall operating account budgets $552,660.

City Hall financing purpose · City Hall account, p. 35 · Debt service, p. 87

Hurricane Michael recovery

The city refinanced its $150 million short-term recovery note in 2025. The original note had replaced $24.5 million in recovery credit lines and helped pay project costs while the city awaited grant reimbursements. The staff refinancing memo lists September 1, 2027 as the maturity date.

How much goes to MLK Recreation Center?

The city budgets $1,254,581 for MLK and $3,713,278 for the separate Parks, Culture and Recreation account. Together they total $4,967,859. MLK represents 25.3% of those two operating budgets combined.

Fire service assessment

How Panama City pays for fire service

This special assessment appears as a separate charge from city property taxes. The city’s fire assessment study also identifies Springfield and Panama City Beach as nearby examples that have used this approach.
