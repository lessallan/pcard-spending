What is a P-card? About these records

Penny Ping analyzes City of Panama City purchasing-card transactions to help citizens understand how the City spends public money. A purchasing card, or P-card, is a City-issued payment card employees use to buy goods and services for City work. These purchases can include supplies, equipment, travel and training, subject to City budgets and purchasing rules.

The records cover purchase dates from August 27, 2024, through September 30, 2026, recorded in fiscal years 2025 and 2026. This is a historical snapshot, not a live view of City spending.

These totals show P-card transactions only, not all City spending or separate expense reimbursements. A cardholder may make purchases for an entire department, so charges associated with a person are not necessarily that person’s own expenses.
