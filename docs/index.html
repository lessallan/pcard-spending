A look back at how your tax dollars were spent

Penny Ping analyzes purchasing-card (P-card) transactions for the City of Panama City to help citizens understand how the City spends public money.

This is a historical snapshot, not a live view. The supplied records cover purchase dates from August 27, 2024, through September 30, 2026, recorded in fiscal years 2025 and 2026 (October 1, 2024–September 30, 2026).

P-card transactions are one part of City spending. Separate expense reimbursements are not included.

PUBLIC MONEY · EVERYDAY CITY WORK

What is a P-card?

A purchasing card is a City-issued payment card employees use to buy authorized goods and services for City work. Using one is a normal part of running a city.

A way to pay

P-cards help staff purchase supplies, equipment and services directly from vendors. Authorized travel and training can also be paid by card, subject to City rules.

Within an approved budget

The City approves budgets. Employees are expected to make purchases within those budgets and purchasing rules. A P-card is a payment method within that process.

A clearer public record

Explore who made purchases, where the money went and how it was recorded. A large total does not establish waste or misconduct.

What these records can—and cannot—tell us

This is a view of recorded P-card transactions, not the City’s entire budget. Approved budget allocations and itemized receipts are not included here, so these totals cannot establish whether a purchase was within budget or what every item was. A cardholder may buy for an entire team. Receipt questions may already have been resolved in records held by the City.
