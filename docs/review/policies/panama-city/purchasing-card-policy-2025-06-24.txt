PURCHASING CARD POLICY MANUAL
FOREWORD
The Purchasing Card (P-Card) Program has been established to  support the departments that
make up the City’s government. To efficiently and effectively manage all aspects of the P-Card
function, the Purchasing Division  has developed this policy manual governing the P -Card
Program.
The P-Card Program is designed to improve efficiency in processing purchases from vendors
that accept Visa payment cards. It allows cardholders to purchase authorized goods and
services directly from vendors without the need for purchase orders.  Each P -Card is issued in
the name of the employee responsible for its use and includes the City’s Tax-Exempt Certificate
number. “City of Panama City” is clearly displayed on the card as the governmental buyer of the
goods and services.
The P-Card may be used to purchase authorized goods and services in person, at the vendor's
location, over the telephone, or online.
This policy is not intended to replace or circumvent standard purchasing practices. P-Cards are
designed to supplement  the City’s purchasing policies and procedures . Best  practices in
purchasing management should always be followed in the procurement of goods and services.
All P-Card purchases shall be made with consideration given to departmental budget limits.
The success of the P-C ard program depends on the adherence to policies and procedures by
the Cardholders, in conjunction with careful and timely review by the department m anagement
and oversight by the Purchasing Division and Accounting Department.

OBJECTIVES
 To provide guidelines/policies for the responsible use of P-Cards
 To provide a framework for the appropriate use of P-Cards for buying goods and
services
 To ensure accountability for misuse of the P-Cards
When changes to this policy manual are necessary, and upon notification by this department of
official changes, new pages will be issued to supplement and update the manual. Department
heads are requested to ensure that all employees are notified of any changes to the purchasing
policies and procedures.

AUTHORIZATIONS
A. P- Cards will be administered by the Purchasing Division. The Purchasing Specialist will serve
as the Purchasing Card Administrator (PCA) under the direction of the Logistics Director and
the City Manager.
B. All requests for new P -Cards must be approved by the employee’s Department Head by
submitting an email request to the PCA. Cards will be issued on an “as needed” basis.
C. Changes to existing card limits may be requested by the Department Head in writing (i.e.
written or electronic) to the PCA.
D. Before a P -Card is issued, each employee must attend a training class conducted by the
Purchasing Division explaining the usage rules. The employee must then read and sign the
acknowledgment and receipt form before being giv en the P -Card. By signing this form, the
employee affirms that he or she has read and is familiar with the rules, regulations, and
policies stated in the P -Card Manual. Employees who abuse or purposely or repeatedly
misuse the P-Card will have their card r evoked and may be subject to disciplinary action up
to and including termination of employment.
E. P- Cards may only be used by the person whose name appears  on the face of the card and
should never be loaned to any other person. The account number shall not be given to any
individual other than the vendor for a purchase. In all instances, the person who is issued the
P-Card is responsible for all charges made with their card.
F. When a purchase authorization is requested by the vendor at the point -of-sale, the P-Card
system validates the transaction against present limits established by the City. All transactions
are approved or declined based on the established purchasing card authorization criteria. The
authorization criteria may be adjusted periodically as needed and may include, but is not
limited to the following:
• Single Purchase Limit
• Monthly Spending Limit
• Approved Merchant Category Codes
• Number of transactions allowed per day
• Number of transactions allowed per month
• Temporary Spending Limit

RESPONSIBILITIES
The following are the responsibilities of:

A. Cardholder
1. Hold and secure your P-Card.
2. Comply with all P-Card policies.
3. Order materials as authorized by your department.
4. Verify that budgeted funds are available before ordering
5. Compare pricing (Office Depot vs. Amazon, Lowes vs. Home Depot, etc.) to ensure you
are paying the least amount for items.
6. Notify vendors of the City’s Sales Tax Exemption before finalizing any purchases.
7. Obtain an itemized sales receipt, invoice, or order confirmation from the vendor.

8. Sign and date the P-Card receipt to indicate the authenticity of the transaction.
9. Provide the receipt to your department’s P-Card Representative within 7 days of the
purchase.
10. Notify the PCA immediately if your P-Card is lost, stolen, or requires cancellation.
11. Use the P-Card for authorized purchases only. P-Cards are not to be used to purchase
personal items.
12. Card Security
• Do not leave your card unsecured
• Do not lend your card
• Do not save your card number on a website
• Do not include your card number in a fax or email
• Keep your card separate from your personal cards
• P-Card account numbers should not be left unsecured or easily accessible to
others
• Do not allow vendors to store your P-Card number

B. Departmental Purchasing Card Representative

non-compliance to the Department Head.
2. Review and reconcile the monthly statement with the cardholder.
3. Update the “comments” section for each transaction on the bank’s website with a
complete description of the item and the City Department's use of it.
4. City’s contract number/project name should be indicated in the “comments” section
for purchases made with contracts.
5. Notify the PCA of any suspicious transactions posted on the bank’s website.
6. Assign and/or verify the appropriate ledger accounts for each transaction on the
bank’s website.
7. Ensure sufficient budgeted funds exist to cover expenditures.
8. Identify disputed items and assist the cardholder with a solution.
9. Ensure P-Card transactions are reviewed and approved on the bank’s website in a
timely manner.
10. Ensure monthly report processing on the bank’s website is completed by the 5th of
each month.
11. Forward monthly reports and supporting documentation electronically to the
Accounting Department by the 5th of each month.
12. Notify the PCA immediately if a P-Card is lost, stolen, or requires cancellation.
13. Collect cards from cardholders who end employment or transfer to another
department, notify the PCA to cancel the card, and return it to the PCA. For
departmental transfers, the new department will determine whether the employee
needs to maintain their P-Card or cancel it if necessary.
14. Review the list of cardholders quarterly with the Department Head to determine if any
cards should be cancelled.

C. Department Head or Designee
1. Set authorization criteria for each cardholder within established guidelines of the
Purchasing Manual.
2. Designate department P-Card representative.
3. Oversee employees' P-Card purchases, requests, and spending limits.

5. File the master statement and all backup documents.

LIMITS AND RESTRICTIONS
A. The P -Card is for official business only. International pur chases, except for Canada, are
restricted to protect against fraud. If international purchases are necessary, contact the PCA
for temporary approval before purchasing.
B. All items paid for with the P-Card are the property of the City of Panama City.
C. Purchases of food and beverages are prohibited except in specific circumstances  as

--- PDF page 7 ---
7
determined by the City Manager or designee. The City Manager or designee shall designate
the cards that will be coded to allow food and beverage purchases . Food for emergency
situations may require special approval based on the cardholders’ code restrictions.
D. Exceptions can be permanent or for a single transaction request. For the transaction to be
allowed, the  PCA must receive a request from the Department Head, City  Manager, or
designee as appropriate and in advance of the purchase so that the cardholder’s merchant
category codes can be changed on the bank’s website.
E. Fuel may only be purchased in the following instance:
1. For City vehicles on authorized City trips outside of Bay County.
2. For an approved rental car on an authorized City trip outside of Bay County.
3. For Undercover Police Officers.
4. For City equipment, emergencies with rental equipment , or unforeseen circumstances
may warrant.
F. The PCA will set the  single purchase limit and the monthly limit based on the following
limitations:
1. The minimum single purchase limit shall not exceed $3,499, and the maximum monthly
limit shall not exceed $10,000 per person unless approved by special exception by the
City Manager or designee.
2. A transaction may consist of multiple items, but its total may not exceed $3,499 without
prior approval of the PCA.
3. Deliberately splitting up a purchase to keep the total below the single purchase limit of
$3,499 is strictly prohibited.
RESTRICTED ITEMS
A. The following types of items may NOT  be purchased on a P -Card, regardless of the dollar
amount:
1. Cash advances.
2. Florida sales tax.
3. Alcoholic beverages and tobacco products.
4. Entertainment expenses.
5. Clothing, even if it is worn as a uniform.
6. Tips or gratuities are not permitted on any P-Card purchases:
a) Except when pre-approved for food purchases (i.e. special events and/or
emergencies), or
b) Except when traveling a traveler may include a tip of up to 20% of the fare
as part of the total fare amount claimed. The tip is not separately
reimbursable. “Fare” means the price paid for transportation, including all
mandatory taxes and fees; it does not include ancillary fees for optional
services. All transportation-related expenses are required to be in
alignment with Federal travel regulations.
7. Incidental expenses such as tips for porters, baggage handlers and hotel staff, etc. are
prohibited and are considered covered by employees per diem.
8. Purchase of goods or services for personal benefit/gain.
9. Recurring maintenance, rental, or lease of equipment that exceeds $1,500 per year,
unless the Purchasing Division receives prior approval.
10. Utilities (i.e., telephone services, cable, electricity).

8
11. Merchant Rebate, purchases for which merchants offer a rebate must be processed
through a purchase order.
12. Travel costs for a City Traveler’s companion (i.e., airline tickets even if the traveler is

13. Travel meals may not be purchased on the P -Card unless a special exception is

17. Any other purchases deemed prohibited by the respective Department Head.

COMPLETING TRANSACTIONS WITH VENDORS
A. Over-the-Counter Purchases
1. When an over-the-counter purchase is made, the cardholder must obtain the
customer’s copy of the charge slip or receipt, which will become part of the
reconciliation document. If the receipt does not designate the item(s) purchased, attach
a detailed listing.
2. All items purchased over the counter must be immediately available. Any items
ordered, but not available, should not be charged to the City’s account until delivery is
made to the City or written confirmation of shipping is received.

B. Telephone/Email Orders
1. Purchases must be described in sufficient detail so that the parties to the transaction
clearly understand what is being acquired. It is strongly advised to follow up a phone
conversation with an email confirming the details of the transaction.
2. Instruct the vendor to send the sales receipt directly to the cardholder and not to send
an invoice to the Accounting Department.
3. Shipping or delivery fees must be included in the unit price.
4. Purchases made in Florida and for use in Florida are exempt from sales and use taxes.
The City’s tax-exempt identification number is printed on the face of the P-Card.
5. Vendors should be instructed that invoicing should not occur until items have been
shipped. Florida law prohibits payment to a vendor prior to receipt of goods or
services except in specific circumstances.
6. All items purchased by telephone or email must be delivered to the City within the 30-
day billing cycle. The order should not be placed without this assurance.
SHIPPING CHARGES
A. Shipping charges may be charged to your P-Card, within reason, considering the cost of the
item itself and urgency by which it is needed.
B. If vendors offer “no shipping fee” deals for dollar amounts, try to take advantage by placing
one order instead of multiple orders.
TAXES

--- PDF page 9 ---
9
A. Most purchases are exempt from sales tax. The tax-exempt identification number is listed on
the face of the P-Card. Cardholders must ensure sales tax has not been added to a purchase.
If a purchase has sales tax, the employee must request that a credit be processed for the
sales tax amount within 15 days.
B. Some internal purchases will require a cardholder to call a customer service number on the
company’s website in order to have taxes removed from the order . It is the responsibility of
the cardholder to ensure that this occurs.
C. Cardholders using a P-Card out of state may be subjected to that state’s tax laws.
D. The City is required to pay sales tax on fuel, airline taxes, and resort/tourist taxes.
BACKORDERS AND DEPOSITS
A. The City will only pay for goods and services received or shipped at the time of purchase.
Backorder items that have been charged to a P-Card must be cancelled by the cardholder.
B. No deposits are to be made on any goods or services that cannot be shipped or delivered
immediately with the P-Card. A deposit indicates a partial payment and is strictly prohibited.
Deposits for hotel reservations are allowed.
C. Any exceptions to this section must be approved by the PCA on a transaction-only basis with
written approval.
LOST OR STOLEN CARDS
A. If a P-Card is lost or stolen, the cardholder must immediately contact the PCA and the bank.
The cardholder must fill out the required affidavit sent to them by the bank.
CHANGES IN AUTHORIZED USERS
A. In the event of termination or transfer, the department is responsible for collecting the P-Card
from the employee and returning it to the PCA as soon as possible. The card will  be
deactivated.
B. If an employee transfers to a different department, the new department may request that the
P-Card be updated and reactivated.
CREDITS AND RETURNS
A. Merchandise returned to the vendor must be credited to the City’s account to which the
transaction was charged. Cardholders are not authorized to receive a cash payment or
store credit for returned merchandise.
B. The user's department is responsible for ensuring that credits for returned merchandise are
properly applied to the monthly account statement.
C. Any promotional, rebate cards, or certificates obtained by any cardholder are to be submitted
to Purchasing.
DISPUTES

--- PDF page 10 ---
10
A. A dispute occurs when a cardholder questions a transaction charged to the Cardholder’s
account. If the purchase was unauthorized, then the transaction MUST BE DISPUTED.
B. It is the responsibility of the cardholder to document, dispute, and resolve erroneous charges
directly with the vendor. In most cases, disputes can be resolved in this manner.
C. If a dispute cannot be resolved, a Statement of Disputed Items must be submitted to the bank
as soon as the dispute is determined, but no later than 60 days after the end of the billing
cycle in which the dispute first appears. The transaction must be marked as a dispute in the
Works Program, and a copy of the dispute form must be sent to the PCA.
D. If items purchased with the P-Card are defective, the cardholder must return the item(s) to the
vendor for replacement or credit. If the service paid for the P-Card is faulty, the vendor must
be notified and asked to correct the situation or  provide a credit. The purchase must be
disputed if the vendor refuses to replace or fix the faulty item or service. The transaction must
be disputed if the quantity of the items received is less than the amount charged on the
invoice.
E. The following steps must be taken to ensure prompt settlement of disputes:
1. If a cardholder finds an unauthorized purchase made using their P -Card information,
they must contact the bank immediately to begin the dispute process.
2. If the item(s) purchased are defective or billed incorrectly, the Cardholder must contact
the vendor and attempt to resolve the issue immediately. If the cardholder and the
vendor cannot come to a resolution, the Cardholder must immediately contact the bank
to have the transaction placed in a disputed status.
3. If a purchase is placed in a disputed status with the bank, the Cardholder must complete
any forms sent by the bank.
4. It is the Cardholder's responsibility to follow through with the vendor or the bank  to
ensure all disputed transactions are resolved in a timely manner.
Note: The Purchasing Division can and will assist if needed
SUSPENSION AND ACCOUNTABILITY
A. Suspension is a progressive policy step and may be put into effect due to:
1. Failure to submit proper documentation to the PCA or departmental P -Card
representative by the established deadlines.
2. Lost receipts.
3. Inappropriate purchases of goods or services.
4. Purchases that  exceed the Cardholder’s established P -Card limits for singl e-item
purchases.
5. Circumventing established single transaction limits by “splitting” purchases into multiple
P-Card transactions.
6. Keeping a rebate or refund from a vendor, bank, or other financial institution for personal
use.
7. Acceptance of a cash refund instead of credit to the P-Card account.
8. Failure to report a lost or stolen P-Card.
9. Purchases from vendors that create conflicts of interest.
B. Depending on the severity of the offense, the first offense could result in permanent revocation
of card privileges. The Cardholder’s Department Head may take disciplinary action beyond
the steps listed here:



--- PDF page 11 ---
11
1. First Offense: The Cardholder will receive a documented, written notification from  the
PCA and their supervisor will be notified.
2. Second Offense: Card privileges will be suspended for one to three months. During
this period, the affected P-Card must be surrendered to the PCA.
3. Third Offense: Permanent revocation of card privileges.
C. Employees who abuse or purposely or repeatedly misuse their P -Card will lose their card
privileges and may be subjected to disciplinary action up to and including termination of
employment. Intentional misuse of a P-Card will be turned over to the Police Department for
investigation.
D. Department Heads will be notified when approvals on the bank’s websites are not completed.
E. Unauthorized purchases shall be reimbursed to the City. Reimbursement may be made
directly to the City or through payroll deduction.

RECORDKEEPING AND RECEIPTS
A. The Cardholder is responsible for obtaining itemized receipts for all purchases. The receipt
must include a description of the item(s) and the amount paid for the purchase. Receipts must
be signed and dated. It is the employee’s responsibility to ensure the description on the sales
receipt is legible and clearly describes the purchase. If the receipt does not clearly describe
the items, you will need to attach a memo describing each item and the purpose for the items.
The departmental P -Card representative will use these receipts to reconci le the monthly
departmental transactions and will be part of the documentation kept on file electronically to
substantiate the payment process. The Cardholder is responsible for submitting all
documentation to their Department P-Card representative within 7 days of the purchase.
B. Documentation must be accurate, accessible, and complete for authenticity purposes, as it is
not only a record of the transaction, but also supports the legitimate public purpose of the
purchase.
1. Priced and itemized invoice
2. Subscription due forms or applications
3. Conference registration forms
4. Statement of service report from vendor performing on- site repairs, including itemized
final pricing
5. Cash register itemized receipts
6. Email with a detailed payment summary
7. Screen print of online order summary
C. When an allowable food or meal has been purchased, the itemized, detailed receipt must
include the names (written down) of the people or group of people who ate and the City event.
D. The Cardholder must make every effort to obtain all required documentation.
1. Contact the vendor to request a receipt.
2. Print a receipt from your online vendor account if purchased online.
E. If all efforts to obtain documentation of a transaction have failed, the Cardholder must attach
a written description  of the purchase along with a signed statement that the purchase was
made following the City’s P-Card Policy. Failure to provide a statement may result in loss of
the card and/or disciplinary action, and the employee may be required to pay the City for the
undocumented expense.
F. Repeated occurrences of lost receipts may result in the suspension of your P-Card.

BILLING STATEMENTS/MONTHLY DEPARTMENT BANK RECONCILIATION
A. The department P -Card Representative will be given access to the bank’s website to
download billing statements, review transactions, and reconcile transactions.
B. Transactions are traditionally available for viewing at the bank’s website within 24 to 48 hours
of purchase.
C. The City’s monthly billing cycle typically runs from the first day of the month through the last
Friday of the month. Monthly billing statements can be downloaded from the bank 48 hours
after the cycle closes.
D. Reconciliation should be ongoing throughout the cycle and not left until the end of the month.
E. The new month’s fund will not appear on the P -Card until the Department Purchasing Card
Representative (Reconciler) has completed and signed off on the previous month’s
reconciliation.
