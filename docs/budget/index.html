Understand the property-tax calculation

Assessed value is the value used before exemptions. An exemption removes eligible value from taxation; taxable value is what remains. A homestead is a qualifying permanent home. One mill means $1 of tax for each $1,000 of taxable value.

The calculator’s baseline is its starting comparison, and each estimate depends on the listed assumptions. Read Florida’s property-tax guide · Read the tax-term definitions

What could you save?

Amendment 3 · Property tax calculator

Florida voters approved Amendment 3 in November 2026. To estimate its effect on your city tax, use the assessed value before exemptions for a qualifying homestead inside Panama City limits. Find it on your property record or notice of proposed property taxes.

Calculation assumptions

All years use the same assessed value and the published 4.7999 mill city operating rate as a reference. The 2026 baseline applies the first $25,000 exemption and up to $26,411 above $50,000. The 2027 and 2028 estimates apply the respective $150,000 and $250,000 exemptions. Estimates cover city operating property tax only and exclude county, school and district levies, assessments and other exemptions.

Will this lower my whole tax bill?

The increased exemption applies to eligible non-school property taxes. School taxes and separate assessments can still appear on the bill, and individual savings depend on your assessed value, eligibility and future rates.

What if I moved to Florida after 2026?

Florida’s Department of Revenue says owners who had not established Florida residency by December 31, 2026 generally become eligible for the expanded exemption in their fifth year of exemption. Check your eligibility with the Bay County Property Appraiser.
