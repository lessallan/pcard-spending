# Assets and places

Use the official park/facility directory, fleet roster and fire-station list when available. Save name/alias sources and distinguish facilities, vehicles, equipment and project names. A unit number is not automatically a vehicle; a compressor on a unit can describe building equipment.

Maintain exact source mentions and separately reviewed aliases. Recognize repeated unit numbers across years, but do not assume the same asset forever: replacement dates, reused numbers and changed departments require review. City directory membership verifies a place exists, not that a particular ambiguous note refers to it.

One invoice can mention multiple parks/units or include supplies unrelated to the named asset. Mark shared/mixed charges, retain the full invoice, and count its ID once in overall totals. Do not divide costs across units without itemized amounts; estimated person allocations are not asset cost allocations.

Separate all associated purchases from repair/service purchases. Fuel, accessories, new equipment and maintenance are different purposes. Show yearly totals, original notes, payers, charged cost centers, confidence and missing confirmation. A charged department is not necessarily the using department. Save exact membership IDs and roster/source evidence in the ledger; keep uncertain types/locations Likely.
