# Refund reconciliation

Keep original signs and full amounts. Rank positive charges separately from net cost, and show potentially related credits alongside large purchases.

Prefer exact booking/ticket/folio references and explicit cancellation notes. Otherwise compare payer, merchant, amount and competing originals together. Same amount alone is insufficient; partial/multiple refunds need a visible residual and itemization. A duplicate-receipt warning does not prove duplicate billing.

A reviewed likely pair can be reconciled financially without assigning a trip. Example: Robinson's $1,298.57 Delta charge and -$1,298.57 cancellation credit net to zero, but differing ticket numbers keep the match Likely and the March session unresolved. Store both records as mutual dependencies. Do not infer the trip date from the refund date, suppress the credit from the ledger, or count a zero-net cancellation as a newly discovered trip.

For each accepted pair/group, save originals, credits, net cents, match evidence, confidence, unresolved balance and required confirmation. Verify a credit isn't allocated to multiple originals. A full canceled booking does not prove an entire trip was canceled. If one side is filtered out, identify the limited scope rather than presenting a false full reconciliation.
