RESOLUTION NO. 20250624.2
A RESOLUTION  OF THE CITY  COMMISSION  OF THE
CITY OF PANAMA CITY DECLARING THE PURCHASING
CARD POLICY MANUAL TO BE ADOPTED, AND
MODIFYING ITS POLICY AND PROCEDURE MANUAL S
TO INCLUDE THE ADDITION.
WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Panama City and its employees desire to
adopt the Purchasing Card Policy Manual;
WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Panama City desire to provide procedures
for the use of purchasing cards by the employees;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED  by the City Commission of Panama City,
Florida, as follows:
Section 1.   The above recitals are true and correct and incorporated in this Resolution.
Section 2.  The Purchasing Card Policy Manual of the City of Panama City , which is
attached as “Exhibit A”, is hereby adopted for immediate use.
Section 3.  The addition of the Purchasing Card Policy Manual is for distribution to all
employees and incorporated in the current Policy and Procedure Manual of the City.
Section 4.  This Resolution shall become effective upon adoption.
PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the City Commission of the City of
Panama City this 24th day of June, 2025.
CITY OF PANAMA CITY, FLORIDA
