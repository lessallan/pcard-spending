## Use and version notes

- PDFs are unchanged downloads. Matching `.txt` files contain searchable text extracted with pypdf, separated by PDF page. Consult the PDFs for layout, forms, and any image-only content.
- `sources.json` records source URLs, retrieval date, byte counts, and SHA-256 checksums.
- The standalone P-Card manual and broader purchasing manual are separate sources. Keep both available; this import does not resolve any differences between their provisions.
- An earlier agenda attachment has a June 10, 2025 cover date. The June 24 version was imported instead, consistent with the adoption minutes.
- The broader manual is the version linked from the Purchasing Division page at retrieval. This does not establish that no subsequent internal amendments exist.
- For FY25/FY26 transaction review, match transactions to the policy effective at the transaction date. These documents alone do not establish the complete policy history before June 24, 2025.
- Existing transaction spreadsheets were not modified or evaluated by this import.
